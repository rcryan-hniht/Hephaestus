Hephaestus

From Blueprint to Reality.

Hephaestus is an AI-powered mobile application that transforms a 2D house blueprint into an interactive 3D structural visualization.

Instead of simply looking at a floor plan and trying to imagine what the building will look like in three dimensions, Hephaestus allows users to point their phone's camera at a blueprint and watch the house grow directly from the drawing.

The goal is simple:

Draw it. Scan it. Watch it become a building.

✨ What is Hephaestus?

Hephaestus is designed for engineering students, architecture-related students, and professional engineers who work with house and building plans.

A traditional 2D blueprint contains a large amount of information, but understanding how those lines translate into a three-dimensional structure can still be difficult.

Hephaestus provides another way to understand the design:

2D Blueprint → AI Understanding → 3D Structural Model

The application reads the blueprint through the phone's camera, sends the captured image to the AI processing system, and reconstructs the building as a 3D model based on what can be interpreted from the drawing.

The system does not attempt to redesign the building or decide whether the blueprint is correct.

Instead, it faithfully visualizes the design so that the person who created the blueprint can judge it themselves.

🎯 The Problem

When designing a building, students and engineers often work primarily with 2D drawings.

While experienced professionals can mentally visualize the final structure, this can be much harder for students or when working with complicated designs.

A drawing may look correct in 2D while its three-dimensional structure reveals something that was difficult to notice from the original drawing.

Hephaestus aims to bridge that gap.

Instead of asking:

"What will this drawing look like in 3D?"

You can simply point your camera at it and see.

🚀 How It Works
1. Scan the blueprint

Open Hephaestus and point the phone's camera at a house or building blueprint.

The initial version focuses on images and physical drawings captured directly through the camera.

2. AI interprets the drawing

The captured blueprint is processed by Hephaestus' AI system.

The AI is designed to understand the structural information represented by the drawing and reconstruct the building accordingly.

3. The building grows from the blueprint

This is the core experience of Hephaestus.

Rather than instantly displaying a finished model, the structure is progressively assembled from the bottom upward.

Walls, floors, stairs, and other major structural forms emerge from the drawing, creating the feeling that the building is physically growing out of its blueprint.

4. Explore the result

Once the construction animation is complete, users can interact with the entire 3D model.

🔄 Rotate the building through 360°
🔍 Zoom in
🔎 Zoom out
👀 Examine the overall structure from different perspectives

The model is intended to remain a representation of the overall building structure rather than a collection of individually interactive components.

🏗️ What Does Hephaestus Reconstruct?

Hephaestus focuses primarily on the structural form of the building.

The visualization may include elements such as:

Walls
Floors
Multiple levels
Stairs
Major openings and structural spaces
Roofs and major architectural forms when they can be interpreted from the blueprint

The purpose is not to create a fully decorated architectural visualization.

Furniture, interior decoration, materials, textures, and other visual details are secondary to the main objective:

Understanding the structure of the building.

🧠 AI Philosophy

Hephaestus is not an automated architectural critic.

It does not attempt to tell the designer:

"This wall is wrong."
"This room should be larger."
"Your staircase is incorrectly designed."
"This blueprint should be changed."

Instead, the AI's responsibility is to reconstruct the building as faithfully as possible based on the information it can read from the blueprint.

The human remains the one who evaluates the design.

This creates a simple relationship:

Blueprint → AI Reconstruction → Human Evaluation

Rather than:

Blueprint → AI Correction

The purpose of Hephaestus is to make the consequences of a 2D design easier for a human to see and understand.

👥 Who Is Hephaestus For?

Hephaestus is primarily intended for:

🎓 Engineering & Architecture Students

Students can visualize the buildings they design and develop a better understanding of how 2D technical drawings translate into 3D structures.

👷 Professional Engineers

Engineers can use the visualization as another perspective for examining a building concept and communicating its spatial structure.

🏠 Anyone Learning Building Design

Hephaestus can also serve as a visual learning tool for people who are still developing their ability to mentally interpret technical drawings.

📱 Current Direction

The current development direction focuses on Android.

The initial workflow is intentionally simple:

Open Hephaestus
      ↓
Point camera at blueprint
      ↓
Capture / recognize the drawing
      ↓
AI reconstructs the structure
      ↓
3D building grows upward
      ↓
Explore the completed model

The first version focuses on camera-based image input rather than requiring users to import professional CAD formats such as PDF, DWG, or other specialized engineering files.

🌟 The Vision

Hephaestus is built around a simple idea:

A blueprint should not only be something you read. It should be something you can see come alive.

A student draws a house.

An engineer creates a plan.

The phone looks at the drawing.

And instead of staring at lines on paper, the user watches those lines become walls, floors, stairs, and a building.

That is the experience Hephaestus aims to create.

🔭 Future Possibilities

The current concept focuses on structural visualization, but the same foundation could eventually support more advanced capabilities, such as:

More complex architectural drawings
More accurate multi-floor reconstruction
Improved recognition of different drawing styles
More detailed structural elements
Additional visualization modes
Support for other types of buildings
Additional input formats beyond camera images

These are future possibilities rather than requirements of the current version.

🛠️ Project Status

Hephaestus is currently under development.

The project is focused on building the core pipeline:

Blueprint Recognition → Structural Reconstruction → 3D Visualization

The AI reconstruction system is being developed specifically for the project, with the long-term goal of making the conversion from 2D building drawings to 3D structural models increasingly accurate and useful.

📌 In One Sentence

Hephaestus is an Android application that uses AI to turn a house blueprint seen through a phone camera into an interactive 3D structural model that grows directly from the drawing.

📖 Why "Hephaestus"?

Hephaestus is the Greek god associated with craftsmanship, forging, fire, and creation.

The name represents the project's central idea:

Taking something that exists as a design and forging it into a tangible form.

From lines on a blueprint to a structure you can see.

That is Hephaestus.
